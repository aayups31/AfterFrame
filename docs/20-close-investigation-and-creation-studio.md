# 20 — Close Investigation and Creation Studio

## Product principle

Research and creation are separate modes.

During investigation, the agent creates evidence, branches, and momentum. When the user decides the case is mature, **Close Investigation** can preserve the case or transform the accumulated work into a finished artifact.

This makes AFTERFRAME both:

- an AI-orchestrated research environment; and
- a manual creative workspace grounded in that research.

## Close flow

The close action begins with a review, not immediate generation.

```text
CLOSE INVESTIGATION

Unresolved branches: 3
Material contradictions: 2
Unverified source locators: 1
User notes not yet used: 7

KEEP OPEN · CLOSE AS WORLD · TIE EVERYTHING TOGETHER
```

The system must not imply intellectual closure when major unresolved issues remain. The user may close anyway.

## Closure options

### 1. Close as case world

Freeze the current trail as a completed version while remaining reopenable.

### 2. Visual documentary script

Create an editable sequence with:

- beat or scene number;
- narrative purpose;
- proposed narration or on-screen argument;
- visual direction, archival target, shot idea, or graphic;
- audio or score intention;
- user theory or note being advanced;
- evidence IDs and exact source locators;
- uncertainty or legal/editorial caveat;
- transition to the next beat.

### 3. Research dossier

A structured record of the case, source map, claims, disagreements, timelines, people, and unresolved leads.

### 4. Essay or video outline

A narrative structure without writing every sentence.

### 5. Director or writer brief

Themes, real-world context, visual references, character research, adaptation choices, sensitivities, and open creative questions.

### 6. Evidence appendix

A production-friendly bibliography and claim-to-source map.

## Source-of-truth rule

The closure agent may use only:

- verified evidence in the case;
- user-authored notes;
- accepted theory assessments;
- explicitly included unresolved or rejected branches;
- approved prior-case connections;
- fresh targeted research initiated and verified during closure.

It may not silently introduce uncited facts from general model memory.

## User controls

Before generation, allow the user to choose:

- target format and approximate length;
- audience;
- thesis or open-ended stance;
- which branches to include;
- whether rejected theories appear;
- whether unresolved contradictions remain visible;
- citation density;
- tone and visual language;
- how much direct writing versus structural scaffolding is desired.

## Manual creation

The creation studio must support a blank or partially generated artifact. A user can:

- drag research into the script;
- write every line manually;
- ask the agent to propose only transitions, visuals, or evidence;
- regenerate one beat without rewriting the whole artifact;
- compare generated versions;
- trace any sentence to its inputs;
- lock user-written passages against AI edits.

## Provenance

Every generated artifact block stores:

- contributing note IDs;
- theory branch IDs;
- evidence and claim IDs;
- source locator IDs;
- generation model and prompt version;
- user edits;
- approval state.

The artifact should visually distinguish sourced factual claims, interpretation, user-authored theory, and creative direction.

## Closure is reversible

Closing creates a versioned milestone. Reopening the case does not destroy the closure artifact. New research can create a later closure version.
