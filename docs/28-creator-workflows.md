# 28 — Recurring Creator Workflows

## Why this document exists

The consumer movie experience proves delight. Recurring creator workflows test whether AFTERFRAME can become infrastructure rather than a one-time demo.

These workflows use the same Movie Investigator and case model. They are not separate products.

## 1. Documentary and video-essay research

### Seed

> I want to make a 25-minute documentary arguing that the mission failed before the first helicopter was hit.

### Investigator behavior

- decomposes the thesis into testable claims;
- builds chronology and causal lanes;
- finds primary accounts, independent histories, maps, and visual assets;
- identifies frequently repeated but single-origin claims;
- separates useful story beats from verified conclusions;
- marks evidence with visual potential;
- preserves counterarguments;
- exports a sourced research dossier and optional visual structure at closure.

### Recurring value

Every new video begins with a new case. Research packs, notes, source preferences, and prior themes carry forward.

## 2. Biopic development

### Seed

> I am developing a biopic about a public figure during one specific period.

### Investigator behavior

- resolves life chronology and contested events;
- maps relationships and institutional context;
- distinguishes contemporaneous evidence from later recollection;
- surfaces letters, interviews, biographies, archives, photographs, and locations;
- marks gaps where dramatic invention would be required;
- tracks which real people are composites or disputed;
- creates a character-world dossier, not a screenplay.

## 3. Book-to-film adaptation research

### Seed

> I am adapting this book and need to understand what can be compressed without breaking the world.

### Investigator behavior

- resolves edition and text identity;
- maps characters, events, locations, and thematic dependencies;
- compares existing adaptations when relevant;
- identifies scenes that carry multiple structural functions;
- links historical or scientific context behind the text;
- separates adaptation proposals from source facts;
- preserves page/chapter locators.

The product must not ingest or reproduce unauthorized full copyrighted books.

## 4. Director / production-world research

### Seed

> Help me build the visual and cultural world for a film set in this place and period.

### Investigator behavior

- researches architecture, costume, technology, social structure, geography, language, and daily life;
- prioritizes visual archives and expert sources;
- creates connection boards between factual detail and creative opportunity;
- highlights anachronism risk;
- outputs a sourced world brief.

## 5. Script fact-check and sensitivity pass

### Seed

> Test these scenes for historical claims, chronology, and unsupported implications.

### Investigator behavior

- extracts explicit and implied claims;
- links each to evidence state;
- flags version, chronology, and attribution problems;
- proposes questions rather than rewriting the script by default;
- records accepted and rejected changes;
- exports a claim audit.

## Shared creator requirements

- project-level case collections;
- reusable source libraries;
- team comments later, not V1;
- import of user-owned notes and permitted files;
- block-level provenance;
- export to Markdown, JSON, PDF dossier, and eventually creative tools;
- no silent alteration of user-authored theories or narrative choices;
- strong privacy defaults for unreleased projects.

## V1 creator validation

Build only two polished creator paths:

1. documentary/video-essay research;
2. adaptation or biopic world research.

Interview at least five people who repeatedly perform one of these workflows before implementing collaboration or enterprise controls.
