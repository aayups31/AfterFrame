Status: checkpoint 04F.1, verified passage preparation.

The canonical plan is `docs/23-final-build-plan.md`. It defines eight major
phases, numbered 0 through 7. Numbered build checkpoints such as 04E.1 and 04F.1
are engineering slices inside those phases, not additional product phases or
percent-complete scores.

Working on Phase 3 does not certify that every earlier research, editorial or
behavioral gate has passed. Those gates must be evidenced before release.

## Remaining work inside Phase 3

1. Durable evidence decisions, semantic support review and claim persistence.
2. Source-origin tracing, independent corroboration, contradictions and
   calibrated uncertainty.
3. Broader lawful source adapters: transcripts/timecodes, edition-aware books,
   archives, and film-cut-aware references as supported by source access.
4. Complete the resumable normalization/extraction/verification executors and
   full research loop, with replanning, coverage checks, stopping rules,
   budgets, provider privacy policy and measured latency.
5. Connect researched directions and theory pressure-testing to branches;
   publish paced findings only through the renderability gate.
6. Integrate the live case experience: original inspection, notes, return,
   corrections, cancellation, closure and reopening.
7. Complete the audited golden pack and shadow-mode breadth evaluation against
   a strong generic research baseline, including unseen movies and source gaps.

These seven work areas are a current execution breakdown, not seven small
commits. They can require multiple checkpoints. Four major phases follow Phase
3; Phase 6 is conditional. No credible calendar or percentage follows merely
from counting them.

## Final release outcome

A user chooses any identifiable movie and a real curiosity. The engine finds
appropriate sources, verifies relevant locations, builds reviewed evidence and
claims, preserves conflicting accounts, and reveals an inspectable trail. The
user reads, thinks, directs research, writes notes and decides what to connect.
The direction console changes the main investigation through researched
branches. A saved case survives interruption, reopening and corrections.

Phase 7 adds deployment and operations proof: authorization, deletion/export,
backup/restore, incident response, adapter health, accessibility, mobile and
keyboard behavior, bounded costs and tested job recovery. Research quality and
retention gates remain necessary; a clean build alone cannot certify them.

The ambition is an investigation engine materially better than a strong generic
research baseline on film research. That superiority is not yet established.
The canonical plan requires blinded comparison and absolute trust floors.

The longer-term company may serve documentary makers and other fields through
specialists built on the same core. The browser companion and further
specialists remain outside Movie Investigator V1 and do not have a fixed final
checkpoint count.
