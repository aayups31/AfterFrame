# 30 — User Research and Validation

## The uncertainty

The main risk is not whether the system can search. It is whether users prefer an investigation enough to return and incorporate it into real work.

## Initial cohorts

Recruit separately:

- deep movie rabbit-hole enthusiasts;
- documentary or video-essay creators;
- screenwriters / film students doing adaptation or biopic research;
- one or two professional researchers for contrast.

Do not merge their feedback into one average user.

## Problem interviews before the product demo

Ask about the last real investigation they performed:

1. What triggered it?
2. Where did they begin?
3. Which source types did they use?
4. Where did they lose time?
5. How did they decide what to trust?
6. Where did notes live?
7. What did they create at the end?
8. Which parts would they never delegate?
9. What would make them repeat the process next week?

Avoid asking whether they “would use an AI movie research tool.”

## Prototype test

Use one curated case. Do not explain the product metaphor first.

Observe:

- time to understand the curiosity prompt;
- first scroll behavior;
- whether source hints are noticed;
- whether the user opens an original;
- whether they form and submit a theory;
- whether the branch change is understood;
- whether they make a note without being instructed;
- where pacing becomes slow or dense;
- whether they know what Close Investigation means;
- what they ask to do next.

## Strong signals

- user loses track of time;
- asks to continue after the scheduled test;
- returns without reminder;
- starts a second film;
- imports an active project;
- asks for an extension or workflow integration;
- pays or requests team access;
- provides source corrections because the case matters to them.

## Weak signals

- “This is beautiful.”
- “This could be huge.”
- “I would totally use this.”
- social likes without case completion;
- long sessions caused by confusion;
- creator interest only in automatic script generation.

## Validation ladder

### Level 1 — Comprehension

Five of six users understand that the direction console changes the case rather than starting chat.

### Level 2 — Engagement

Median active case time exceeds 20 minutes for the curated case, excluding idle time.

### Level 3 — Continuation

At least 30% begin or request a second case within seven days in a small invited cohort.

### Level 4 — Workflow pull

At least five creators import or begin research for a real upcoming project.

### Level 5 — Willingness to pay

Users choose a paid pilot or place a card for a clearly defined recurring plan. Do not infer willingness from a pricing survey alone.

## Weekly learning review

Record:

- strongest observed behavior;
- strongest failure;
- what was believed before the test;
- what changed;
- one product decision;
- one question left open.

Use `templates/weekly-learning-review.md`.
