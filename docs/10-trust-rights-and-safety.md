# 10 — Trust, Rights, and Safety

## Factual integrity

The interface is cinematic, but the evidence layer cannot become theatrical.

Never show fake actions such as:

```text
Reading 7 books…
Cross-checking 43 reports…
```

unless those operations actually occurred and are recorded in the research run.

## Source hierarchy

Context-dependent, but generally distinguish:

- primary record;
- first-hand account;
- contemporary reporting;
- peer-reviewed or scholarly analysis;
- later historical synthesis;
- production interview;
- community interpretation;
- unsourced commentary.

Do not imply that “primary” means unbiased or correct.

## Contradictions

The system should preserve unresolved disagreements. It may explain:

- different vantage points;
- time elapsed before recollection;
- institutional incentives;
- differing editions or cuts;
- source dependency;
- translation differences.

## Movie spoilers

Cases support:

- no spoilers beyond watched point;
- film spoilers allowed, historical outcome hidden;
- full spoilers;
- production-only exploration.

## Copyright

Design for transformation, minimal quotation, attribution, and original-source traffic.

Avoid:

- reproducing book chapters;
- storing full unauthorized transcripts;
- bypassing paywalls;
- presenting generated reconstructions as archival material;
- using film stills or posters without an appropriate license.

## Defamation and contested real people

For living persons or allegations:

- use high-quality sourcing;
- distinguish allegation, charge, finding, and opinion;
- provide dates and jurisdiction;
- preserve denials and material contrary evidence;
- avoid sensational visual treatment.

## User data

- notes private by default;
- explicit sharing boundaries;
- deletion of notes, cases, derived embeddings, and playlists;
- export in readable and structured formats;
- cross-case memory opt-out;
- no training-use assumption without clear policy and consent.

## Observability

Record:

- model and prompt version;
- tool calls;
- search queries;
- source retrieval status;
- locator verification status;
- claim revisions;
- beat provenance;
- user redirections;
- connection decisions.

This is necessary for debugging both factual errors and bad pacing.
