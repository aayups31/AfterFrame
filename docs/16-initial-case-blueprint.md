# 16 — Initial Case Blueprint

## Film

**Black Hawk Down**

## User curiosity

> Why did everything go wrong?

## Interpreted objective

Trace how a mission expected to be short became an extended urban battle, separating immediate operational failures from deeper assumptions, constraints, and political context.

## Suggested opening sequence

### Beat 1 — Opening

> It was not planned as a battle.

### Beat 2 — Initial assumption

Establish the expected mission duration and objective with book/report evidence.

### Beat 3 — First question

> What had to be true for that timetable to work?

### Beat 4 — Plan dependencies

Target intelligence, helicopter insertion, convoy extraction, communications, and rapid exit.

### Beat 5 — Turn

> The plan did not fail in one place. Its dependencies began failing together.

### Beat 6 — Spatial evidence

Mogadishu road network, barricades, navigation, convoy separation.

### Beat 7 — Contradiction

Compare official and participant accounts about communication and command awareness.

### Beat 8 — Helicopter loss

Explain how a short raid changed category after aircraft were downed.

### Beat 9 — User fork

```text
Follow the tactical cascade
Follow the political constraints
Follow the Somali perspective
```

Free-text direction remains available.

## Minimum curated source pack

- one authoritative historical account/book;
- one official or archival report;
- two first-hand interviews from different roles;
- one high-quality historical analysis;
- one map or geospatial source;
- one film production source, only if the user branches there.

## Notes seeded for UX testing

- “The plan depended on speed more than resilience.”
- “Were communications a technical problem or a command-structure problem?”
- “The helicopter loss changed the mission objective.”

These are mock notes only. The production agent must ground each factual implication.

## Required direction-console demonstration

At Beat 9, submit:

> I think the mission was already fragile before the first helicopter went down.

Expected behavior:

1. temporary acknowledgement: “Whoa—let me get in on that.”;
2. root trail recedes;
3. a child theory branch opens in the main screen;
4. the theory is preserved verbatim;
5. the branch separates support, pressure, contradiction, alternatives, and unknowns;
6. an adversarial research pass tests whether hindsight is overstating pre-existing fragility;
7. the user can return to the exact prior reading position.

## Required close-investigation demonstration

The closure review should show unresolved branches and weak locators, then offer:

- close as case world;
- visual documentary script;
- research dossier;
- video/essay outline;
- director/writer brief.

The visual-script mock should contain at least three beats with narration, visual direction, evidence labels, theory origins, and caveats.
