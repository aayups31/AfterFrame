# 23 — Final Build Plan

## Product sequence

Build in the order that proves the distinctive interaction before the expensive autonomy.

## Milestone 1 — Premium mocked movie case

Deliver:

- cinematic portal;
- curiosity intake;
- paced investigation stream;
- exact-source inspector UI;
- note capture;
- direction console;
- a mocked branch transition;
- close-investigation modal;
- mocked visual-script preview.

Success test:

> The user understands that typing changes the investigation rather than starting a chat.

## Milestone 2 — Curated research truth layer

Use one deeply curated film case and one theory branch.

Deliver:

- verified film version record;
- 20–40 source records across multiple source classes;
- exact locators;
- claim graph;
- theory lanes;
- source independence groups;
- closure artifact with end-to-end provenance.

Success test:

> Every factual or interpretive conclusion can show its evidence, limitations, and origin.

## Milestone 3 — Specialized movie investigator

Deliver:

- movie research axis router;
- film-source scout;
- film-text ingestion for user-provided frames, notes, scripts, and permitted clips;
- version/cut resolver;
- production and creator research adapters;
- theory pressure-testing;
- evaluation suite.

Success test:

> The system behaves materially better on movie questions than a generic deep-research prompt.

## Milestone 4 — Adaptive branching

Deliver:

- live direction classification;
- branch creation and return;
- targeted research jobs;
- stable insertion into the stream;
- branch support-state updates;
- cross-case connection proposals.

Success test:

> Two theories about the same film produce visibly different evidence trails and assessments.

## Milestone 5 — Creation studio

Deliver:

- closure review;
- visual documentary script;
- research dossier;
- outline and director brief;
- block-level provenance;
- manual-first editor;
- versioning and export.

Success test:

> A creator can turn the case into usable work without losing the research trail.

## Milestone 6 — Multiple film archetypes

Validate on:

1. historical adaptation — `Black Hawk Down`;
2. ambiguous interpretation — `The Shining` or `Hereditary`;
3. science and production — `Interstellar`;
4. mythology/adaptation — `Troy`;
5. real-person investigation — `Zodiac` or another carefully sourced case.

Success test:

> The ontology generalizes without becoming a generic topic engine.

## Milestone 7 — Work While I’m Gone

Only after foreground trust metrics pass.

Deliver:

- mission authorization;
- background queue;
- cost and source budgets;
- checkpointing;
- provisional autonomous branches;
- return digest;
- cancellation and audit trail.

Success test:

> A user returns to meaningful, verified progress—not a large generated report.

## Non-goals until later

- automated final video rendering;
- social network or public theory marketplace;
- universal non-film research;
- ingestion of unauthorized full films or books;
- autonomous publishing;
- gamified completion;
- fake productivity metrics.
