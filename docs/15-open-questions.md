# 15 — Product Questions to Resolve Through Prototypes

These are not blockers for the first build.

1. Does the “case” vocabulary feel premium or too literal after repeated use?
2. How often should the investigator interrupt without becoming annoying?
3. Should the first beat begin immediately, or should the user confirm the interpreted intent?
4. How much paraphrase can appear before users feel the product is another AI article?
5. Are source hints always visible or revealed on proximity?
6. Does a persistent direction console reduce immersion, and should it recede completely after a branch opens?
7. Should notes live beside passages during reading or remain hidden until summoned?
8. What is the cleanest way to show contested evidence without using comparison cards?
9. How should cross-case suggestions appear without feeling invasive?
10. Should a user be able to choose “source-first” before beginning?
11. Which playlist integrations are worth the authentication and provider restrictions?
12. What is the minimum source quality required before a beat may stream?
13. How do we communicate unavailable copyrighted source passages without breaking immersion?
14. Does the case world need free spatial placement, or can beautiful automatic layout suffice initially?
15. What visual treatment distinguishes user conclusions from agent-generated interpretations?
